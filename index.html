End-to-End Workflow

The life of a ticket.

From initial customer intake to automated resolution and post-resolution insights.

Stage 01

Ticket raised

Customer submits an inquiry via self-service portal, email, or chat with zero manual routing required.

Stage 02

Categorised

NLP extracts entities, detects intent, and tags billing, technical, or access issues in under 120ms.

Stage 03

Prioritised

Critical revenue outages and VIP accounts automatically jump to Priority 1 before agents even open the queue.

Stage 04

Summarised

Multi-paragraph customer rants are distilled into a 2-line executive digest so agents save 80% reading time.

Stage 05

Reply suggested

A fact-grounded response is prepared and waiting with 1-click tone adaptation (Friendly, Formal, or Short).

Stage 06

Status tracked

Customers inspect progress at any time while managers track resolution health on the executive board.
