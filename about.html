KNOWLEDGE BASE

Frequently answered questions.

Everything you need to know about AI safety, privacy, integrations, and setup.

How does AI triage ensure customer data privacy and GDPR/SOC2 compliance? ▾

BizAssist AI processes inquiries ephemerally using zero-retention API models. Customer PII (passwords, credit cards, SSNs) is masked client-side before ingestion. We offer dedicated data-residency VPCs for Enterprise customers across the EU and US.

Can human support agents edit or override AI-generated suggested replies? ▾

Yes. Every suggested reply is purely a draft. Agents retain full control with one-click tone adaptation (Empathetic, Concise, Technical), rich text formatting, variable insertion, and manual edits prior to dispatch.

How long does it take to integrate BizAssist AI with Okta, WhatsApp, and existing webhooks? ▾

Initial setup takes under 15 minutes. Okta SAML 2.0 connects via standard metadata XML paste, and WhatsApp Business API syncs via webhooks with zero custom infrastructure required.

What happens if our inbound ticket volume experiences an unexpected 10x spike? ▾

Our serverless edge dispatcher buffers inbound requests with exponential retry queues. Automated self-service deflection deflects repetitive FAQs, while the SLA risk board prioritizes critical enterprise tickets automatically.

Can we train or ground the AI on our internal documentation and past resolutions? ▾

Yes. You can connect your Markdown documentation, Notion databases, or Zendesk export. BizAssist creates vector embeddings that ground drafts in verified documentation with citation badges.

What is the difference between Starter, Team, and Enterprise SLA commitments? ▾

Starter includes best-effort normal queues (8h). Team provides guaranteed 1h High SLA response. Enterprise guarantees 15m P1 Urgent turnaround with dedicated SRE on-call escalation and phone callbacks.
